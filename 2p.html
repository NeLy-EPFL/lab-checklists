If something's off

  • Black image: all 3 shutters open (MaiTai, ThorImage laser, PMT)? PMT gain > 0? A tripped PMT reboots at gain 0.
  • Weak / no 2P signal: wavelength 930 nm? fine tuning matches the scope?
  • No SyncData: Remote connection ticked? ThorSync running?
  • Camera missing in octacam: right USB port? uv run octacam doctor
  • 2P_2 bright-field camera green but won't open: close all software, unplug camera, replug, reopen ThorImage. Still stuck: reboot Windows with the camera unplugged.
